Aquecimento: Past Perfect

Antes de avançar, vamos garantir que o Past Perfect está bem assimilado. Lembre-se: usamos had + past participle para falar de uma ação que aconteceu antes de outra ação no passado.

💡 Lembrete rápido: Past Perfect = had + verbo no particípio passado. Exemplo: "When I arrived, she had already left."

Present Perfect: conectando passado e presente

O Present Perfect é usado para falar de ações que começaram no passado e continuam relevantes agora, experiências de vida, ou mudanças recentes. É um dos tempos mais usados no inglês do dia a dia.

Estrutura afirmativa

Subject + have/has + past participle
  • I / You / We / They have worked
  • He / She / It has worked

Negativa e interrogativa

have/has + not + p.p. | Have/Has + subj + p.p.?
  • She hasn't finished yet.
  • Have you ever been to Japan?

Quando usar

  • Experiências de vida: "I have traveled to Peru."
  • Ações recentes: "She has just called."
  • Situações que continuam: "We have lived here for 5 years."
  • Mudanças: "Prices have gone up."

Palavras-chave

  • already, just, yet — ações recentes
  • ever, never — experiências
  • for, since — duração ("for 3 years", "since 2020")
⚠️ Cuidado com a diferença: Present Perfect não tem tempo específico definido ("I have visited Paris"), enquanto o Simple Past sim ("I visited Paris in 2019"). Past Perfect fala do "passado do passado" — sempre em relação a outro momento passado.

Texto: "A Big Change"

Leia o texto abaixo e identifique os verbos no Present Perfect (destacados em negrito).

Maria has lived in São Paulo for ten years. She has never traveled outside Brazil, but this year everything has changed. She has just bought her first plane ticket to Portugal.

Her English has improved a lot since she has been studying with a private teacher. She has already learned the present perfect, and now she has started practicing irregular verbs every day.

"I have never felt so excited about a trip," she said. "I have prepared my documents, and I have finished packing my suitcase. I haven't slept well because I'm so nervous!"

Her friends have asked her many questions about the trip, and she has answered all of them with a big smile. This has been the best year of her life so far.
