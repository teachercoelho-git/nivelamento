📌 Quick Grammar Tip: Going to for Plans

Use "going to" to talk about travel plans you already decided!

✅ I am going to visit Paris next month.
✅ She is going to book a hotel room.
✅ We are going to pack our luggage tomorrow.
❌ I going to travel. ← Missing "am"!
