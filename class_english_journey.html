🏡 My Life and My Routine

Daily life, family, friends and routines.

15-20 minutesEasy EnglishAt your pace
Lesson progress0%

♡ A gentle beginning

You do not need to be perfect today. A few quiet minutes for yourself are enough.

1. Words for today

family memberfamiliar
familyfamília
homecasa
morningmanhã
eveningfim do dia
restdescansar
Read each word aloud twice. Slow is perfectly fine.

2. One small grammar idea

Use Present Simple for routines and facts.
I work. • I live with my family. • We have dinner together.
With he/she, usually add -s: He works. She likes.

3. Easy practice

Choose an answer. You will receive feedback immediately.

1. I ___ with my family.
2. My friend ___ in the morning.
3. We ___ dinner together.
4. I ___ in the evening.

4. Listen and repeat

Follow these three easy steps:

  1. Click Listen.
  2. Listen once without speaking.
  3. Click again and repeat the exact sentence.
My name is Alex.
Meu nome é Alex.
I live with my family.
Eu moro com meu familiar.
In the morning, I have breakfast.
De manhã, eu tomo café da manhã.
In the evening, I rest at home.
No fim do dia, eu descanso em casa.
Full model:
My name is Alex. I live with my family. In the morning, I have breakfast. In the evening, I rest at home.

5. A few words from me

Copy the model and change only the words in bold:
My name is Alex. I live with my family. In the morning, I have breakfast. In the evening, I rest at home. I feel happy when I spend time with my family.

You may copy the model exactly if it is true for you.

Useful English for work and busy days.

2. One small grammar idea

Use can + simple verb for abilities: I can help customers.
Use can’t when something is not possible: I can’t join the meeting today.

3. Easy practice

Choose an answer. You will receive feedback immediately.

2. I ___ help my team.
3. She can ___ customers.
4. I can’t ___ late today.

I can help customers.
Eu consigo ajudar clientes.
Today, I need to finish my work.
Hoje, eu preciso terminar meu trabalho.
Full model:
I work with a team. I can write emails. I can help customers. Today, I need to finish my work.
