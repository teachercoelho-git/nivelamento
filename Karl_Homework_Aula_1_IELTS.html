Parte 3 • Accuracy clinic

A. The important are to have a budget-friendly trip.
The important thing is to have a budget-friendly trip. Também é possível: What is important is to…
B. I passed two weeks in Toronto.
I spent two weeks in Toronto.
C. He is watching the serie in Portuguese.
He is watching the series in Portuguese. “Series” has the same form in the singular and plural.
D. I like to play competitive games and immersive games.
I like playing competitive and immersive games.
E. I like the main characters they are witty, and funny.
