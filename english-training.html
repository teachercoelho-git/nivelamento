Lesson 1 — Age

How to Say Your Age

❌ Common mistake

"I have 26 years old."

Direct translation from Portuguese. Does not exist in English!

✅ Correct

"I am 26 years old."

Or simply: "I'm 26." — both are natural and correct.

🔑 The Rule

In English, age uses the verb TO BE (am/is/are). In Portuguese you say "ter" (to have), but in English we say "I AM 26", not "I have 26".

Lesson 1 — Practice

Say It Right! 🗣️

I have 30 years old. → I am 30 years old. / I'm 30.
She has 22 years. → She is 22. / She's 22 years old.
My boss have 45 years. → My boss is 45 years old.
I have 26. → I am 26. / I'm 26.

💬 Now say it out loud: "My name is [your name] and I am [your age] years old."

Lesson 2 — Politeness

Using "Would Like"

The polite way to express desires, requests, and wishes in professional settings.

Less polite (casual)
  • • "I want coffee."
  • • "I want to meet you."
  • • "I want more information."
✅ Polite & Professional
  • • "I would like a coffee."
  • • "I would like to meet you."
  • • "I would like more information."
💡 Tip — Contractions

I'd like = I would like (spoken / informal writing). Example: "I'd like to schedule a meeting."

Lesson 2 — Practice

Transform These Sentences 🔄

Make it polite: "I want to open an account."
"I would like to open an account."
Make it polite: "I want to know the exchange rate."
"I would like to know the exchange rate."
Make it polite: "I want to invest in bonds."
"I'd like to invest in bonds."
Make it polite: "I want more details about the interest rate."
"I would like more details about the interest rate."
Lesson 3 — Vocabulary

Improve vs Better

Using improve sounds more professional and specific than just saying better.

BETTER — adjective / adverb

Describes a state of being superior

  • • "The results are better this month."
  • • "She feels better today."
IMPROVE — verb (more powerful!)

Describes an action of making something better

  • • "We aim to improve our results."
  • • "I want to improve my English."
❌ Less precise: "I want to make my skills better."
✅ More professional: "I want to improve my skills."
Lesson 3 — Practice

Use Improve Like a Pro 💼

My English is getting better. → My English is improving. ✨
We need to make the process better. → We need to improve the process.
The company's numbers got better. → The company's numbers improved.
I want to get better at communication. → I would like to improve my communication skills.
