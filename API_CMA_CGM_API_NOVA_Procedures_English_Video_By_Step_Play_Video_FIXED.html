Opening script

Pitch for the first meeting

Before discussing technology, I would like to understand how your team works today.

Customers who speak with us usually want to reduce manual tasks, improve shipment visibility or integrate logistics information directly into their systems.

My objective today is to understand your processes and assess whether CMA CGM APIs can automate part of this flow securely and efficiently.

Recommended tone

Consultative, objective and business-oriented. Avoid starting with technical details.

Expected outcome

Prioritized pain point, current flow, system used, stakeholders and success criteria.

Access and organization

Open the correct console and organize List Views

1. Open eCommerce

  1. In the upper-left corner of NOVA, click the dotted square app selector.
  2. Select eCommerce to open the correct console.
  3. Open the Leads tab.
  4. Use the eCommerce Sales LATAM RO queue, where the records arrive.

2. Review the Leads queue

The view should allow records to be tracked with the following statuses New, Working and Qualified.

  • New: still requires a first action.
  • Working: contact or follow-up in progress.
  • Qualified: has progressed and may generate an Opportunity.

3. Create personal List Views

  • Create a List View with your name to see only your cases and Leads.
  • Create a dedicated view for New Leads.
  • When taking ownership of a new Lead, use Change Owner.
  • Then track it in your own List View.

Lead management · API & EDI Direct Connection

From portal registration to a qualified opportunity

Use this workflow to make every Lead traceable, owned and commercially actionable. The sequence below consolidates the current Lead Management training while separating confirmed steps from points that still require team validation.

Pending user→Activate→Find & review→Claim→Working→Contact & meeting→Minutes & follow-up→Account & contact→Convert

1. Pending user & activation

  1. Open API Cockpit → Pending Users and review the company name, email and registration date.
  2. Forward the pending-user details to ho.APIUserValidation@cma-cgm.com to trigger the activation process.
  3. Do not activate the user manually. After customer activation, the Lead is automatically created in NOVA.
To confirm: owner of the activation, exact information required in the request, and escalation path for urgent activation.

2. Find and review the Lead

  1. In NOVA, open Leads → List Views and filter for API-related Leads, or use Global Search with the company/contact name.
  2. Review Lead Name, ID, email, phone, company, product, source, business unit, status and Qualification Rating.
  3. Confirm the Lead belongs to the correct region and product scope before taking ownership.
To confirm: the exact ownership checks before claiming or reassigning a Lead.

3. Claim, describe and set to Working

  1. Use Change Owner and assign the Lead to yourself, or reassign it to the appropriate colleague.
  2. Add a concise description with the known customer context and registration information.
  3. Change Lead Status from New to Working before the first customer contact.

Never leave an actionable Lead without an owner. Working status signals that commercial treatment is in progress.

4. First contact

  1. Send the welcome email from Outlook.
  2. Acknowledge the registration and understand the customer's current ERP/TMS environment and integration need.
  3. Introduce the applicable API journey and propose a discovery meeting when useful.

Recommended subject: Next Steps for Exploring CMA CGM's API Solutions.

5. Create and record the meeting

  1. From the Lead Activity area, create a New Event.
  2. Use Category: Outbound call, Subject: API presentation and Topic: E-commerce.
  3. Set date/time, link the customer contact and save. The event should appear in Upcoming & Overdue.

Use the same event after the meeting to add Minutes instead of creating a second meeting record.

6. Add Minutes and the next action

  1. Open the saved event and select Add Minutes.
  2. Record Topic: E-commerce, Subtopic: API, discussion comments and outcome: Positive, Mitigating or Negative.
  3. Create a follow-up Task with subject Follow up, assigned to yourself, with the agreed due date and Reminder Set enabled.
Good practice: end the meeting with a clear customer next step and date, then use that date for the NOVA follow-up.

7. Validate Account, Treatment Level and commercial ownership

  1. Search the customer company and identify the correct Compliant / active account. Use city/address to distinguish duplicates.
  2. Review Treatment Level, account organisation/hierarchy and the Account Team.
  3. For Premium/Advanced accounts, identify the relevant CAM/GCAM or sales contact and involve them in the discussion before progressing.
To confirm: the exact validation required for Account, Account Organisation, GCAM and Treatment Level, and whether eCommerce can continue to own/manage a Lead when another commercial owner exists.

8. Link Business Partner and customer Contact

  1. In Lead Qualification, enter the Business Partner code; if no result appears, search by company name.
  2. Select the correct Compliant account, checking Billing City where needed.
  3. Create or link the External Contact with first name, last name, email and Account Name.
  4. Once Business Partner and Contact are linked, the Convert action becomes available.

9. Convert only when commercially qualified

Do not use the availability of the Convert button as the qualification criterion. Convert when the Lead has genuine business potential and should become an Opportunity. If the customer will not proceed, close it as Disqualified.

Must clarify before V2 is standardized: the minimum business criteria that define when a Lead is sufficiently qualified to become an Opportunity.

Opportunity creation and management

From Account to eCommerce Opportunity

1. Find the Account

  1. Leave the qualified Lead record.
  2. Search for the company name in the top search bar.
  3. Open Contacts, locate the customer, and open the Account.
  4. Confirm in the upper-left corner that the page shows Account, not only Contact.

2. Create the Opportunity

  1. In the Account, click New eCom Opportunity.
  2. Select the offer API.
  3. Associate the contact name.
  4. After saving, use the green box displayed to open the created Opportunity.

3. Follow up

In the customer Account, review the Opportunities under the Sales. Update the stage, comments, next steps and customer decision.

4. Close the decision

When the customer confirms whether to proceed, move the Opportunity to the applicable closing stage.

Validate terminology: Closed, Closed Won, Closed Lost ou “Disclosed”.

Operational gap: a List View for Opportunities, or an equivalent view, should be created, including a way to revisit Lost Opportunities. The best configuration should be validated with the team.

Meeting governance

Record the Meeting and then create the Minute

Create the Meeting

  1. Search for the customer email address in NOVA’s upper-right search bar.
  2. Open the corresponding Contact.
  3. On the right side, click the pink calendar.
  4. Select Meeting at Customer.
  5. Complete the meeting Subject.
  6. Under Topic, select eCommerce and move it to Chosen using the right arrow.
  7. Enter the date and time, then save.

Create the Minute

  1. Open the Meeting created as an event.
  2. On the right side, click Create Minute.
  3. Record the discussion, needs, decisions, owners and next steps.
  4. Review and validate the Minute.

Points to validate

  • Official link to view all Minutes.
  • Mandatory Minute fields.
