Full dialogue

As falas estão em inglês e foram organizadas em blocos visuais para facilitar leitura em voz alta e escuta guiada.

Karl, the dentist Ronaldo, the patient
Karl

Good morning! I’m Dr. Karl. How have you been?

Ronaldo

Good morning, doctor. I’ve been fine, thanks, but my teeth have been hurting a little.

Karl

I see. How long have you had this pain?

Ronaldo

I’ve had it for about three days.

Karl

Have you taken any painkillers?

Ronaldo

Yes, I’ve taken some, but they haven’t helped much.

Karl

Have you visited a dentist this year?

Ronaldo

No, I haven’t. I’ve been very busy with work.

Karl

Have you ever had problems with this tooth before?

Ronaldo

Yes, I’ve had a small cavity there once, but it hasn’t hurt like this before.

Karl

Have you been brushing and flossing regularly?

Ronaldo

I’ve brushed my teeth twice a day, but I haven’t flossed every day, to be honest.

Karl

Have you changed your toothpaste recently?

Ronaldo

Yes, I’ve started using a whitening toothpaste. I’ve used it for about two weeks.

Karl

That might be part of the problem. Some whitening toothpastes have caused sensitivity in other patients.

Ronaldo

Really? I haven’t noticed any issues until this week.

Karl

Have you drunk anything very cold or very hot today?

Ronaldo

Yes, I’ve had some iced coffee this morning, and it’s made the pain worse.

Karl

All right. I’ve checked your X-ray, and I’ve seen a cavity under your old filling.

Ronaldo

Oh no… So what have you decided to do?

Karl

I’ve decided that we should replace the filling today. It’s a simple procedure.

Ronaldo

Have you done this treatment many times?

Karl

Yes, I’ve done it hundreds of times. Don’t worry, it’s quick and safe.

Ronaldo

That’s good. I’ve always been afraid of dentists, but you’ve made me feel calmer.

Karl

I’m glad to hear that. Have you eaten anything in the last two hours?

Ronaldo

No, I haven’t eaten anything since breakfast.

Karl

Perfect. I’ve prepared the instruments and I’ve already called the assistant. We can start now.

Ronaldo

Thank you, doctor. I’ve waited too long to fix this tooth.

Karl

After the treatment, you may feel a little discomfort, but it shouldn’t last long.

Ronaldo

How long have your other patients usually felt the discomfort?

Karl

Most of them have felt it only for one or two days.

Ronaldo

Great. I’ve wanted to take better care of my teeth for a long time.

Karl

That’s a good decision. You’ve made the first step today. We’ll talk about a new routine after the procedure.

Ronaldo

Thank you. I’ve appreciated your help and your patience.

Teacher note

  • Use o botão de áudio para o aluno ouvir o diálogo inteiro primeiro.
  • Depois toque apenas a fala do Karl ou apenas a do Ronaldo para shadowing.
  • Peça para o aluno repetir pausando entre as falas e identificar as estruturas no Present Perfect.
