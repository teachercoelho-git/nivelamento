Pro Strategies

Why You're Not Fluent Yet — and How to Fix It

You're translating in your head
Your brain still runs: Portuguese → English before speaking. Fix: think in pre-built chunks. "I was wondering if..." is one pre-stored unit, not 5 words.
Shadowing rewires your muscle memory
Listen and repeat simultaneously. Focus on rhythm and stress, not perfection. 10 minutes daily beats 2 hours on weekends, every time.
Fillers buy you time — and sound native
Natives say "You know what?", "I mean...", "It's like..." to think out loud. Using these sounds fluent. Silence sounds awkward.
Speak first, then think
Stop waiting for the perfect sentence. Start talking before you know how you'll finish. Native speakers adjust mid-sentence constantly — it sounds confident.
