1. Quick Review

Subject and object pronouns

SubjectObjectExample
ImeI like Anna. Anna likes me.
youyouYou help Tom. Tom helps you.
hehimHe knows Julia. Julia knows him.
sheherShe calls Pedro. Pedro calls her.
ititThe dog is cute. I love it.
weusWe visit Grandma. Grandma visits us.
theythemThey see the children. I see them.
Subject pronoun comes before the verb: She likes music.
Object pronoun comes after the verb or a preposition: I like her. / Talk to him.

The “S” with he, she and it

I / You / We / They
I work.
They play.
He / She / It
He works.
She plays.
Remember: In affirmative sentences, add -s to the verb with he, she, it. But after does or doesn't, use the verb without S: Does she like pizza? / She doesn't like pizza.

How to make questions

Do + I/you/we/they + verb...?
Do they study English?

Does + he/she/it + verb...?
Does she study English? Not: Does she studies...?
