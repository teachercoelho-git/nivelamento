Creative Work: Present Perfect vs Simple Past

60-minute online lesson with clickable answers, typing tasks, speaking, and homework

🔥 Warm up📘 Learn🧩 Choose⌨️ Type🎤 Speak✅ Finish

Learning target

Choose between Present Perfect and Simple Past when talking about creative work, experiences, and finished events.

Success criteria

  • At least 8/10 in the main online practice
  • Four spoken experience questions with past follow-ups
  • A six-sentence mini-profile
0–7 min

Warm-up

Answer aloud or type short notes.

  1. What did you design last week?
  2. Have you ever created content for a brand?
  3. When did you start working as a graphic designer?
  4. Have you posted anything online today?
7–17 min

Visual language focus

Simple PastPresent Perfect
subject + past verb
I designed a logo yesterday.		subject + have/has + participle
I have designed many logos.
Finished time: yesterday, last week, in 2025, ago.Experience or connection to now: ever, just, already, yet, since, for.

Online strategy: Click the time clue in your mind. If the time is finished, choose Simple Past. If the experience or result connects to now, choose Present Perfect.

39–47 min

Type the corrected sentence

Type your correction, then reveal a model answer to compare. Equivalent correct wording is acceptable.

I have posted the video last night.
Model answer: I posted the video last night.
Did you ever design a website?
Model answer: Have you ever designed a website?
She hasn't sent the invoice yesterday.
Model answer: She didn't send the invoice yesterday.
i have already finish the project, but i didn't emailed the client yet.
Model answer: I have already finished the project, but I haven't emailed the client yet.
47–54 min

Speaking interview

Answer each question aloud. After each answer, add a Simple Past detail with When?, Where?, or What happened?

  1. Have you ever created a project you were extremely proud of?
  2. Have you ever learned a new design tool by yourself?
  3. Have you ever changed your content style?
  4. Have you ever worked under a very short deadline?
54–60 min

Writing and exit ticket

Write six sentences titled My creative journey: two Present Perfect, two Simple Past, one with already/yet, and one with since/for.

Exit 1: I ___ the artwork yesterday.
Exit 2: She ___ the draft yet.

Homework 20–25 min

Complete online. Use the check button only after answering all six items.

Rewrite

Correct: I have met the client yesterday.

Correct: She didn't finished the design yet.

Join with since: I started using this software in 2023. I still use it.

Mini content diary

Write 80–100 words about a recent creative project, using at least three examples of each target tense.
