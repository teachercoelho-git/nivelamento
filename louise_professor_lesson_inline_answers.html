Creative Work: Present Perfect vs Simple Past

60-minute professor version with inline correct answers, typing tasks, speaking, and homework

🔥 Warm up📘 Learn🧩 Choose⌨️ Type🎤 Speak✅ Finish

Learning target

Choose between Present Perfect and Simple Past when talking about creative work, experiences, and finished events.

Success criteria

  • At least 8/10 in the main online practice
  • Four spoken experience questions with past follow-ups
  • A six-sentence mini-profile
0–7 min

Warm-up

Answer aloud or type short notes.

  1. What did you design last week?
  2. Have you ever created content for a brand?
  3. When did you start working as a graphic designer?
  4. Have you posted anything online today?
7–17 min

Visual language focus

Simple PastPresent Perfect
subject + past verb
I designed a logo yesterday.		subject + have/has + participle
I have designed many logos.
Finished time: yesterday, last week, in 2025, ago.Experience or connection to now: ever, just, already, yet, since, for.

Online strategy: Click the time clue in your mind. If the time is finished, choose Simple Past. If the experience or result connects to now, choose Present Perfect.

17–39 min

47–54 min

Speaking interview and feedback

  1. Have you ever created a project you were extremely proud of? Follow-up: When did you create it?
  2. Have you ever learned a new design tool by yourself? Follow-up: Which tool did you learn?
  3. Have you ever changed your content style? Follow-up: Why did you change it?
  4. Have you ever worked under a very short deadline? Follow-up: What happened?
Feedback: Praise the experience + specific past detail pattern. If the learner uses Present Perfect with a finished time, point to the time clue and elicit the correction before giving it.
54–60 min

Writing and exit ticket

Six-sentence task as in the student version.

I ___ the artwork yesterday.finishedYesterday is a finished time.
She ___ the draft yet.hasn't approvedYet marks a pending result connected to now.
Writing feedback protocol: Mark one strong tense choice, underline no more than three repair points, ask for self-correction, then provide the model. Check capital I, punctuation, and complete subjects.
