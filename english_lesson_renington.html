English Training Session for Renington

Level: Intermediate

Topic: Organization and Training Skills

Goal: By the end of this lesson, Renington should be able to organize tasks, prepare training sessions, and train colleagues in his department and other departments using English confidently.

1. Key Vocabulary

Workflow = Sequence of tasks and processes.
Procedure = Official way of performing a task.
Trainer = Person who teaches others.
Onboarding = Training new employees.
Knowledge Transfer = Sharing knowledge with others.
Best Practices = Most efficient working methods.
Standardization = Creating a consistent process.
Continuous Improvement = Making processes better over time.

2. Professional Expressions

  • Let me walk you through the process.
  • Please feel free to ask questions.
  • The objective of this training is...
  • Let's review the procedure step by step.
  • Can everyone follow this process?
  • This approach helps improve efficiency.
  • Let's discuss the best practices for this activity.

3. Reading Activity

A company is implementing a new operational process. Renington has been assigned to train employees from different departments. Before conducting the training, he organizes the learning material, creates a step-by-step guide, and schedules practice sessions. During the training, he encourages participation, answers questions, and provides real-life examples. As a result, employees quickly adopt the new process and improve productivity.

Discussion Questions

  1. Why is organization important before training people?
  2. What techniques make a training session effective?
  3. How can a trainer ensure that employees understand the process?

4. Quiz

Question 1: What is a workflow?

A sequence of tasks and activities required to complete a process.

Question 2: What is the purpose of knowledge transfer?

To share expertise and information with other employees.

Question 3: Which is more professional?
A) Just do it this way.
B) Let me explain the recommended process.

B) Let me explain the recommended process.

5. Speaking Practice

  • How to receive a request.
  • How to analyze the information.
  • How to complete the task.
  • How to report the results.

6. Trainer Challenge

  1. The purpose of today's training is __________.
  2. The first step of the process is __________.
  3. If you have any questions, __________.
  4. The main benefit of this procedure is __________.

7. Final Roleplay

You are leading a training session for employees from another department.

  • Welcome participants.
  • Explain the objective.
  • Present the process.
  • Answer questions.
  • Confirm understanding.

Suggested Opening:

Good morning everyone. Thank you for joining today's training session. The objective of this training is to help you understand our process and apply it effectively in your daily activities. Please feel free to ask questions throughout the session.

Lesson Outcome

  • Use professional training vocabulary.
  • Explain procedures clearly in English.
  • Conduct onboarding and training sessions.
  • Answer questions confidently.
  • Train teams from different departments.
