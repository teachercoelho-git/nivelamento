Pitch for the first meeting

Before discussing technology, I would like to understand how your team works today.

Customers who speak with us usually want to reduce manual tasks, improve shipment visibility or integrate logistics information directly into their systems.

My objective today is to understand your processes and assess whether CMA CGM APIs can automate part of this flow securely and efficiently.

04 · LEADS

Leads

Manage API and EDI Direct Connection leads from portal registration through qualification, ownership, customer contact and conversion.

Pending user→Lead→Working→Qualified→Opportunity
Lead management · API & EDI Direct Connection

From portal registration to a qualified opportunity

Use this workflow to make every Lead traceable, owned and commercially actionable, from user activation through opportunity conversion.

Pending user→Activate→Find & review→Claim→Working→Contact & meeting→Minutes & follow-up→Account & contact→Convert

1. Pending user & activation

  1. Open API Cockpit → Pending Users and review the company name, email and registration date.
  2. Forward the pending-user details to ho.APIUserValidation@cma-cgm.com to trigger the activation process.
  3. Do not activate the user manually. After customer activation, the Lead is automatically created in NOVA.

2. Find and review the Lead

  1. In NOVA, open Leads → List Views and filter for API-related Leads, or use Global Search with the company/contact name.
  2. Review Lead Name, ID, email, phone, company, product, source, business unit, status and Qualification Rating.
  3. Confirm the Lead belongs to the correct region and product scope before taking ownership.

3. Claim, describe and set to Working

  1. Use Change Owner and assign the Lead to yourself, or reassign it to the appropriate colleague.
  2. Add a concise description with the known customer context and registration information.
  3. Change Lead Status from New to Working before the first customer contact.

Never leave an actionable Lead without an owner. Working status signals that commercial treatment is in progress.

4. First contact

  1. Send the welcome email from Outlook.
  2. Acknowledge the registration and understand the customer's current ERP/TMS environment and integration need.
  3. Introduce the applicable API journey and propose a discovery meeting when useful.

Recommended subject: Next Steps for Exploring CMA CGM's API Solutions.

5. Create and record the meeting

  1. From the Lead Activity area, create a New Event.
  2. Use Category: Outbound call, Subject: API presentation and Topic: E-commerce.
  3. Set date/time, link the customer contact and save. The event should appear in Upcoming & Overdue.

Use the same event after the meeting to add Minutes instead of creating a second meeting record.

6. Add Minutes and the next action

  1. Open the saved event and select Add Minutes.
  2. Record Topic: E-commerce, Subtopic: API, discussion comments and outcome: Positive, Mitigating or Negative.
  3. Create a follow-up Task with subject Follow up, assigned to yourself, with the agreed due date and Reminder Set enabled.
Good practice: end the meeting with a clear customer next step and date, then use that date for the NOVA follow-up.

7. Validate Account, Treatment Level and commercial ownership

  1. Search the customer company and identify the correct Compliant / active account. Use city/address to distinguish duplicates.
  2. Review Treatment Level, account organisation/hierarchy and the Account Team.
  3. For Premium/Advanced accounts, identify the relevant CAM/GCAM or sales contact and involve them in the discussion before progressing.

8. Link Business Partner and customer Contact

  1. In Lead Qualification, enter the Business Partner code; if no result appears, search by company name.
  2. Select the correct Compliant account, checking Billing City where needed.
  3. Create or link the External Contact with first name, last name, email and Account Name.
  4. Once Business Partner and Contact are linked, the Convert action becomes available.

9. Convert only when commercially qualified

Do not use the availability of the Convert button as the qualification criterion. Convert when the Lead has genuine business potential and should become an Opportunity. If the customer will not proceed, close it as Disqualified.

05 · OPPORTUNITIES

Opportunities

Manage the complete API Opportunity journey in NOVA, from a qualified Working Lead through package configuration, quotation, DocuSign and pipeline monitoring.

Working Lead→Convert→Digital Connection Offer→Manage Packages→New Quote→API Agreement→DocuSign→Quotation
Before conversion, the Lead must have both Business Partner and Contact linked. The conversion date becomes the Opportunity creation date.
Opportunity creation and management

From Working Lead to signed API agreement

Follow the sequence below in NOVA. Verify each record before moving to the next stage.

1. Validate and convert the Lead

  1. Open the Working Lead and confirm that Business Partner and Contact are linked.
  2. Click Convert in the top-right corner.
  3. Confirm that NOVA creates and opens the Opportunity.
  4. Note the Opportunity creation date and copy the Account Name exactly when needed later.

Pipeline stages shown in the process: Qualification → Quotation → Negotiation → Closed.

2. Set the Digital Connection Offer

  1. Open Opportunity Information.
  2. Edit Digital Connection Offer.
  3. Select Digital Customer for API packages, or Charge Code Customer for DIC01 billing.
  4. Save the Opportunity.

Digital Customer unlocks Manage Packages. The Charge Code path skips package configuration.

3. Add and configure packages

  1. Click Manage Packages and select each agreed package.
  2. For every package, set the consumption limit, verify the price and choose the agreed APIs.
  3. Set Invoice Frequency. Monthly is the standard shown in the process and the field must not be left blank.
  4. Click Next for each package, then Finish.

Price is per package, not per selected API. Standard tiers are Starter, Power and Advanced; Mini packages use 2,500 or 5,000 units; Custom values are entered manually.

4. Verify Products and API Products

  1. Review the Products section after finishing package configuration.
  2. Check Product Name, Product Code, Price, Invoice Frequency, Consumption Limit and Quota Call per Hour.
  3. Review the individual endpoints in API Products.
  4. Use Manage Packages again if corrections are required before creating the Quote.
Known issue in the source: Visibility Plus (RTV) may not appear in the generated API contract. Flag it to Jessica if missing.

5. Create and save the Quote

  1. In the Opportunity, scroll to Quotes and click New Quote.
  2. Confirm the format Quote - [Account Name] - API - [DD/MM/YYYY].
  3. Select the authorised signatory as Contact. If missing, create an External Contact with first name, last name and email.
  4. Confirm the Account and save. NOVA assigns the Quote Number automatically.

Leave Invoice Frequency blank on the Quote because it is already defined per package.

6. Generate and verify the API agreement

  1. From the Quote, click API commercial agreement.
  2. Wait for NOVA to generate the pre-filled .docx document.
  3. Use Preview or Download and verify company name, registered address, contact email, offer totals and selected APIs.
  4. If any data is wrong, correct it in NOVA and regenerate the document.

For an amended contract, download and edit the Word file, start Send for Signature, delete the preloaded file in DocuSign and upload the edited version.

7. Configure and send through DocuSign

  1. Open Send for Signature and confirm the document.
  2. In Recipients, verify the signatory and customise the email subject and message.
  3. In Prepare & Send, place the required Signature, Initials, Date, Name, Email, Company and Job Title fields.
  4. Send the envelope.

After sending: Opportunity stage = Quotation, Quote status = Presented and DocuSign status = Sent for Signature.

8. Monitor and close the Opportunity

  1. Use the saved API Opportunities view in NOVA and filter by stage.
  2. When DocuSign confirms completion, verify that the Quote status becomes Accepted.
  3. Continue with the Service Contract and deployment steps in the corresponding sections of this guide.
  4. After completion, move the Opportunity to Closed Won.
Traceability rule: keep stage, products, customer decision and next action current throughout the process.

    Q&A · OFFICIAL API PORTAL FAQ

    API Portal FAQ mirror

    A navigable internal mirror of the official CMA CGM API Portal FAQ for quick reference. Use the source link below whenever you need to confirm the latest wording or operational condition.

    Official source: Faq - API Portal - CMA CGMContent mirrored into this guide from the public FAQ.

    Booking Requests

    Can I provide only CMA Business Partner code without party details?

    It is strongly recommended to also provide the booking parties name, addresses and contact details, including the booking-party email address.

    Can I retrieve my booking data through API?

    Yes. Booking data can be retrieved by booking reference through shipping.shipment.v3. The response can also provide data needed for Shipping Instruction.

    Can I be notified when a booking is confirmed or cancelled?

    Yes. With Shipment Event, prepare a webhook to receive events pushed by CMA CGM. CMA CGM web-account notifications may also be available.

    Which party is mandatory in the Booking Request API?

    The booking party with the correct party code is mandatory. Provide parties.contact.emailAddress so booking confirmations and information reach the right destination.

    Is preparation required before Booking Request goes live?

    Yes. UAT sign-off or sandbox testing is required before go-live.

    When can I amend a booking?

    After the booking request is successfully created and has a CMA CGM booking reference. After Shipping Instruction confirmation, subsequent changes should be requested as an SI amendment.

    What can be amended in a booking request?

    All booking-request content can be amended except the booking party and electronicCustomerReference.

    When can I cancel a booking?

    After the booking is successfully created with a CMA CGM booking reference. Depending on operational progress, consult the booking agency before cancellation.

    What does a 502 upstream-server error usually mean?

    The API is usually temporarily unavailable. Outside planned maintenance, report the issue to b2becustomersupport@cma-cgm.com.

    Client Management

    What is the Main User role?

    A privileged role that can retrieve API secret credentials using 2FA and approve or manage user requests linked to the client.

    How do I manage users linked to my client?

    A Main User can open My Organization > Client Settings to review and approve user requests, manage existing users and oversee commercial contacts.

    How can I retrieve or reset my Client Secret?

    A Main User can open My Organization > Client Settings > API Credentials to view the current secret or generate a new one. Two-factor authentication may be required.

    Events

    What is the difference between an API and Event Push?

    With an API, the customer calls CMA CGM when data is needed. With Event Push, CMA CGM calls the customer webhook when an event occurs.

    Can I use different webhooks for different events?

    No. The FAQ states that one single webhook is used for all events.

    Can I receive events only for selected bookings?

    Yes. The Event Subscription API can be used to select which bookings should generate events.

    How many events are pushed to the webhook each time?

    One event is pushed to the webhook each time.

    Can CMA CGM provide the Push server IP for firewall filtering?

    No. The FAQ says the Push server IP cannot be provided and does not recommend IP filtering on the customer side.

    Free Trial access

    Which APIs are available in Free Trial?

    The FAQ lists Schedules APIs (Lines & Services, Routing Finder, Voyages), Logistics Tracking service API, and Referential APIs including Commercial Lines & Services, Goods Codification, Location, Packaging, Shipment and Tracking Events, Shipping Company and Vessel referentials.

    Invoices

    How am I billed for API usage?

    The FAQ marks HTTP 200, 201, 204, 206 and 404 as chargeable. It marks 400, 401, 403, 429, 500, 502, 503 and 504 as non-chargeable.

    LOA

    Why do we need an LOA?

    An LOA provides formal written authorization for CMA CGM to share a customer’s data with a third party.

    Who needs to sign an LOA?

    A CMA CGM client requesting that its data be shared with a third party that is not party to the booking needs an LOA.

    What tool is used for electronic signature?

    DocuSign is used for electronic signatures.

    What is client mapping?

    Client mapping identifies the customer entity in CMA CGM systems and defines which entity allows which data to be shared, and with whom.

    Pricing Contracts

    Which contracts are returned from the API?

    The returned contracts depend on the offices or business-partner codes selected in scope. Applicable contracts where a selected office is a deciding party are returned.

    Can FMC and non-FMC contracts be retrieved together?

    Yes, when applicable to the search criteria, both may be returned in the same API response.

    What does 200 OK with an empty array mean?

    For applicable public quote-line searches, it means no valid quotation was found for the search criteria on the simulation date.

    What does includedInBasicFreight: true mean?

    The charge is rolled into BASIC FREIGHT, so the individual charge amount is null because it is already included in basic freight.

    Referential

    How can I get a full list of HS commodity codes?

    Use the CMA CGM Goods Codification Referential API. The FAQ recommends the CMA CGM referential rather than external commodity-code lists.

    Can I have access to all bookings?

    No. Access is restricted to parties involved in the booking, such as consignee, deciding party, freight forwarder, notify parties, requester, shipper and third-party booking agent.

    Shipment

    Can the Shipment API find a not-yet-confirmed or cancelled booking?

    The Shipment API retrieves confirmed or cancelled bookings. shipmentStatus in the response shows the booking status.

    Where can I get container codes?

    Use the CMA CGM Packaging Referential API.

    Is UAT required for Shipment API?

    No. Shipment API retrieves data rather than submitting it, so UAT is not required.

    Shipping Instruction

    Does the API support split or merge BL?

    Split is supported through the splitBooking endpoint. Merge is not supported at the time reflected by the FAQ.

    Does the API support House BL, Part-Load BL or VGM submission?

    The FAQ says House BL and Part-Load BL are not supported, and VGM submission is not supported at the time reflected by the FAQ.

    Which business party is mandatory for Shipping Instruction?

    Shipping Instruction Requestor (REQ) is mandatory with a valid partner code and email address. For US bookings, Forwarder (FOR) is also mandatory.

    Which business party is not allowed in Shipping Instruction?

    Booking party (BKG) is not allowed.

    Can I submit SI before I have all container numbers?

    No. containerNumber is mandatory, so the FAQ instructs the user to wait until all container numbers are available.

    Are non-English characters accepted in SI?

    No. The FAQ says only English characters A-Z and a-z are accepted.

    Does the API support UN/LOCODE for ports?

    Yes. Ports can use CMA CGM internalCode or external locationCodifications such as UN/LOCODE.

    Track and Trace

    What is the maximum number of events returned in one API call?

    The default maximum is 100 events. The limit parameter can increase this to 500 per call. For more than 500, use pagination with the cursor from the next-page header.

    Why might no Shipment event be returned?

    Shipment events are returned only when the customer is a business party involved in the booking. Otherwise, only Equipment and Transport events are returned.

    Why can a valid booking reference return 404 Not Found?

    The FAQ says this happens when the booking has not been confirmed or has already been cancelled.

    Why can /events and /events/{trackingReference} return different results?

    /events is DCSA compliant. /events/{trackingReference} is primarily for backward compatibility and provides more detailed commercial-cycle information.
