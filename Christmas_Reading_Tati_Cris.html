T

Tati's Christmas

Reading 1
Tati is not planning to travel this year, although she will be on vacation in December. She intends to celebrate Christmas with her mother, grandmother, cousins, and other relatives. Her family does not usually exchange presents at Christmas. Instead, they focus on enjoying one another's company, sharing a delicious meal, and spending quality time together. Each person brings a dish or something to eat, and everything is placed on the table for everyone to share. For Tati, the celebration is less about gifts and more about creating warm memories with the people she loves.

Useful expressions

be on vacation exchange presents enjoy one another's company spend quality time bring a dish
C

Cris's Christmas

Reading 2
Cris normally has Christmas Eve dinner with her husband's family on December twenty-fourth. The following day, she usually has lunch with her own family. Their meals often include traditional Brazilian Christmas dishes, such as roast Chester, rice, and Brazilian-style potato salad with mayonnaise. Last year, her family organized a Secret Santa gift exchange. However, it was not an annual tradition, and they still have not decided whether they will do it again this year. Regardless of the final plan, Cris looks forward to gathering around the table and celebrating with both sides of the family.

Useful expressions

Christmas Eve the following day traditional dishes Secret Santa both sides of the family

Conversation Practice

A conversation between Tati and Cris

TatiCris, have you made any plans for Christmas yet?
CrisMore or less. On Christmas Eve, we usually have dinner with my husband's family, and on Christmas Day, we have lunch with my family. What about you?
TatiI am not travelling this year, but I will be on vacation in December. I am going to celebrate with my mother, grandmother, cousins, and the rest of the family.
CrisThat sounds lovely. Do you exchange presents?
TatiNot really. Instead of buying presents, everyone brings something to eat. We put all the dishes on the table and share everything.
CrisI like that idea. It probably makes the celebration feel more relaxed and meaningful.
TatiExactly. The most important thing for us is being together, eating, talking, and having fun. What do you usually eat?
CrisWe normally have Chester, rice, potato salad with mayonnaise, and other traditional Brazilian Christmas dishes.
TatiDo you also have a Secret Santa gift exchange?
CrisWe did one last year, but we have not decided whether to do it again this year. We will probably make that decision closer to Christmas.
TatiWhatever you decide, I hope you have a wonderful time with both families.
CrisThank you! I hope you have a joyful and relaxing Christmas, too.
