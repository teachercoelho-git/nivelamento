🎯 Ice Breaker: "Guess Who?" (5 minutes)

🎲 How to Play:

Each student shares 3 facts about a family member (without saying who they are). Others guess the relationship!

Example: "This person is older than me, she loves cooking, and she's my dad's wife!" → Answer: Mother/Mom! 👩‍🍳

🎤 Teacher Prompts:

"Think about someone in your family..."

"What do they like to do?"

"How are they related to you?"

😄 Fun English Facts!

💡 In English, we use "siblings" for brothers AND sisters together!

💡 "Aunt" and "Uncle" can be married or siblings of your parents!

💡 British say "Mum" 🇬🇧, Americans say "Mom" 🇺🇸 - both are correct!

💬 Family Dialogue Practice (10 minutes)

Sarah: 🙋‍♀️

"Hi Maria! Tell me about your family!"

Maria: 🙋‍♀️

"Well, I have a big family! My mother is a teacher and my father is a doctor."

Sarah: 🙋‍♀️

"That's interesting! Do you have any siblings?"

Maria: 🙋‍♀️

"Yes! I have one brother and two sisters. My brother is the oldest."

Sarah: 🙋‍♀️

"Wow! And what about your grandparents?"

Maria: 🙋‍♀️

"They're wonderful! My grandmother makes the best cookies, and my grandfather tells amazing stories!"

🎭 Now it's YOUR turn!

Practice this dialogue in pairs. Then, change it to talk about YOUR real families!

🗣️ Speaking Challenge: "My Family Story" (10 minutes)

🎤 Activity Instructions:

Each student presents their family for 2-3 minutes!

Use the prompts below to help you! 💪

📝 Useful Phrases:

"I'd like to tell you about my family..."

"I have ___ people in my family."

"My favorite family member is ___ because..."

"We like to ___ together."

"My ___ is very ___." (adjectives: kind, funny, smart, caring)

🎯 Speaking Prompts:

1️⃣ How many people are in your family?

2️⃣ Who is the funniest person in your family? Why?

3️⃣ What do you like to do with your family?

4️⃣ Who do you look like in your family?

5️⃣ What's a funny family tradition you have?

🌟 Bonus Challenge!

Can you describe a family member using 5 adjectives? Try it! 🎯

Example: "My sister is smart, creative, funny, kind, and very tall!"
