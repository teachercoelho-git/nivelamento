Part 3 - Reading Comprehension

Read: Maria was asked to train employees from three different departments. Before starting the training, she prepared a presentation, organized the training materials, and created a checklist of activities. During the session, she encouraged participation and answered all questions. After the training, employees felt confident using the new process.

  1. How many departments did Maria train?
  2. What did Maria do before the training?
  3. How did she encourage learning?
  4. What was the result of the training?

Part 4 - Professional Expressions

Rewrite each informal sentence in a more professional way.

  1. Just follow my instructions.
  2. If you don't understand, ask me.
  3. This is the best way.
  4. We need to talk about the process.

Part 5 - Speaking Practice

Prepare a two-minute speech: How to Train a New Employee.

Useful language: Good morning everyone. / The objective of today's training is... / Let me walk you through the process. / Please feel free to ask questions. / Thank you for your participation.

Part 6 - Scenario Exercise

Imagine you are training a colleague to use a new system. Write 80-120 words explaining what the system does, why it is important, how to use it, and common mistakes to avoid.

Part 7 - Leadership Challenge

  1. What makes a good trainer?
  2. Why is organization important before training?
  3. How can you keep participants engaged?
  4. What should a trainer do when someone has questions?
  5. How do you know if training was successful?

Bonus Activity

Write an email inviting colleagues to a training session. Include the date, time, purpose, expected benefits, and contact information.
