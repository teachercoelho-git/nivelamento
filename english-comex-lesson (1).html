Etapa 1 — Warm-Up

🌡️ Ice Breaker — Discussion Starters
Responda em inglês com seu parceiro ou anote suas ideias. 2–3 minutos.
  • Have you ever had a job interview in English? How did it go?
  • What foreign trade documents do you know? Name at least 2 in English.
  • What countries does Brazil trade with most? Say it in English!
  • Describe your ideal job in foreign trade using 3 words in English.

Interview Opening Script
Pratique a pronúncia destas frases-chave
"Good morning. Thank you for the opportunity to interview for this position. My name is [Name], and I have been working in international trade for two years. I'm particularly experienced in import and export procedures, customs clearance, and logistics coordination. I'm confident that my skills in foreign trade documentation align well with your company's needs."

Tip for students: When introducing yourself, always mention your area of expertise, one specific skill, and express enthusiasm. Avoid just saying "I am [Name] and I studied foreign trade."

Etapa 5 — Simulated Interview

💼 Perguntas de Entrevista Simulada

Pratique responder perguntas reais. Escreva ou diga sua resposta, depois revele o modelo ideal.

Objetivo: desenvolver respostas estruturadas usando o método STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result).

⭐ The STAR Method
S — Situation
Set the context. Where were you? When?
T — Task
What was your responsibility?
A — Action
What did YOU specifically do?
R — Result
What was the outcome? Use numbers!

📅 Day 1 — Written Task

Your Professional Profile

Escreva um parágrafo de apresentação profissional em inglês, como se fosse para uma entrevista de comércio exterior. Imagine que você é candidato(a) a uma vaga de Foreign Trade Analyst.

  • 1Write 80–100 words introducing yourself, your background, and your skills in foreign trade.
  • 2Use at least 5 vocabulary terms from today's lesson (e.g., customs clearance, supply chain, Incoterms...).
  • 3Include one specific achievement or experience using the STAR method.
  • 4Proofread for grammar: check subject-verb agreement and use of past tense.
📊 Rubric (10 points)

Vocabulary use (3pts) · Grammar accuracy (3pts) · Clarity and organization (2pts) · Use of STAR method (2pts)

🎤 Day 2 — Oral Task

Recorded Mock Interview

Grave um vídeo ou áudio de 2–3 minutos respondendo a 3 perguntas de entrevista escolhidas por você. Use seu celular ou computador.

  • 1Choose 3 questions from the Simulated Interview section of today's class.
  • 2Record yourself answering each question in English. No reading from paper!
  • 3Watch your recording and note: eye contact, pace, vocabulary usage, filler words ("uhm", "like").
  • 4Write a self-assessment (3–5 sentences): what went well, what to improve.
📊 Rubric (10 points)

Fluency & confidence (3pts) · Vocabulary range (3pts) · Answer structure/STAR (2pts) · Self-assessment quality (2pts)
